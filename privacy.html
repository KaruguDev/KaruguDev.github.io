Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

"n8n automation" is a personal tool used only by its owner, Paul Kimani Karugu. The only Google account connected to it is his own.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: it creates draft emails and reads the status of the threads those drafts belong to (whether they were sent, deleted or replied to). It does not read other email.
  • Google Sheets: it reads and writes one spreadsheet that records job applications.

Where the data goes

Data stays in the owner's Google account and on his own n8n server. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and it is not transferred to anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
